Execution Methodology

Our Strength. Our Process. Our Commitment.

Stage-wise engineering governance delivering end-to-end quality and on-schedule handover.

01

Consult & Site Survey

Rigorous joint site survey, 3D laser interference checks, foundation readiness audits, and tender scope alignment.

02

Design, WPS & PQR

Formulating approved shop drawings, welding procedure specifications (WPS/PQR under AWS D1.1), and rigging lift plans.

03

Procure & Rapid Mobilize

Raw material sourcing with Mill Test Certificates (IS 2062) and dispatching ACE 15T Hydras and crew within 7–10 days.

04

Fabricate & Erect

High-tolerance structural fit-up, certified multi-pass welding, heavy girder placement, and 100% daily PPE compliance.

05

NDT & Hydro-Test

100% Ultrasonic Testing (UT), DPT, Sa 2.5 blast profile gauges, and pressure hydro-testing up to 40 bar cleared by TPAs.

06

Deliver & Warranty

Shipping Release Note (SRN), complete as-built dossiers, coating warranties, and long-term maintenance support.
