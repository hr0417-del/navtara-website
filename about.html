Enterprise Profile

A Legacy of Industrial Mastery

Navtara Projects Pvt. Ltd. stands at the intersection of deep engineering expertise and real-world industrial execution. Founded by Mr. Amit Kumar Singh, a professional with experience in Metso Outotec and large-scale industrial erection and commissioning, and Ms. Jayanti Chauhan, an MBA in Banking & Finance, the company is built on a foundation of technical mastery and strategic vision.

We bring together a high-performance team of engineers, designers, and technicians to deliver robust, precision-driven heavy engineering solutions that solve complex industrial challenges with efficiency and reliability.

At Navtara, we go beyond conventional project delivery — we become long-term partners in our clients' growth journey. Every solution we design is aligned with the principles of durability, sustainability, and operational excellence, ensuring that industries not only perform today but are ready for tomorrow.
