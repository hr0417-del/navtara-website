Tender Prequalification

EPC Technical Prequalification FAQs

Direct technical criteria, code certifications, inspection benchmarks, and mobilization protocols for procurement and tender evaluation committees.

All structural fabrication and welding strictly conform to AWS D1.1 (Structural Welding Code - Steel) and ASME Section IX. Navtara maintains a dedicated pool of 40+ certified welders qualified in 3G, 4G, and 6G positions, re-tested under third-party supervision every 6 months. Approved Welding Procedure Specifications (WPS) and Procedure Qualification Records (PQR) are maintained for SMAW, GMAW/MIG, and FCAW across IS 2062 (E250/E350) and boiler-grade alloy steels.

Quality audits are executed by certified ASNT & ISNT Level II inspectors. We perform 100% Visual Testing (VT), 100% Ultrasonic Testing (UT) on critical full-penetration groove welds, Dye Penetrant Testing (DPT), and Magnetic Particle Inspection (MPI). Our quality dossiers and stage-wise inspection hold points are regularly audited and cleared by leading Third-Party Inspection Agencies including Bureau Veritas (BV), TÜV Rheinland, RITES, DNV, SGS, and Engineers India Ltd (EIL).

We execute abrasive grit blasting to Sa 2.5 (Near-White Metal) per ISO 8501-1 / SSPC-SP 10 with surface profile roughness maintained between 50 to 75 microns. Coatings are applied with Graco Xtreme airless spray equipment. Every batch includes digital environmental logs (relative humidity, dew point spread >3°C, ambient temp), continuous wet film thickness checks, calibrated electronic dry film thickness (DFT) reports, and cross-cut/pull-off adhesion testing adhering to high-corrosion C4/C5-M specifications.

Navtara owns and deploys fleet machinery including ACE 15T Hydra mobile cranes, 400A/500A inverter welding units, 750 CFM screw compressors, copper slag blast pots, and certified scaffolding banks. With pre-established regional hubs in Gujarat, Odisha, Maharashtra, and Chhattisgarh, our advance mobilization teams and lead supervisors deploy to brownfield or greenfield sites within 7 to 10 days of Letter of Intent (LOI) / Work Order issuance.

Navtara operates with comprehensive statutory governance: GSTIN (09AAKCN7407P1ZP), PAN (AAKCN7407P), UDYAM (UP 500274423), and EPFO (UPLKO3681930000). 100% of deployed technicians are covered under ESIC, Group Workmen Compensation (WC), and site-specific Contractor All Risk (CAR) policies. We maintain an exemplary Zero Lost Time Incident (0 LTI) safety track record enforced by mandatory daily Tool-Box Talks (TBT), Job Safety Analyses (JSA), and full PPE compliance.
